A SMALL MANIFESTO

why a snorse?

Because coordinating a household, club, collective, or friend group should not require surrendering the group to another platform.

the best group software is present when asked and nearly invisible the rest of the time.

Signal is already the room

People already talk there. A bot can live at the edge without asking everyone to install another app, remember another password, or feed another engagement graph.

boring is a feature

SQLite. Explicit scopes. Read-only calendars. Small commands. Durable volumes. Manual upgrades. Technology chosen to be understandable and recoverable.

capacity should be honest

The public host does not pretend to be infinitely elastic. It measures itself, pauses admission safely, preserves existing groups, and shows aggregate status.

exit doors matter

Shared service is convenience; self-hosting is autonomy. Both are first-class paths. The source and state contract make leaving possible.

the name

A snorse has the speed to show up when called and a shell to disappear into when the job is done. That is the product philosophy: useful on command, quiet by default, and never trying to optimize your soul.
